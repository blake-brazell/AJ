What Is Apple Juice?

Apple juice is a popular drink made by extracting the liquid from fresh apples. It has a sweet taste and can be enjoyed cold or at room temperature.

Apple juice is available in many forms, including fresh, filtered, cloudy, and concentrated juice.

Benefits of Apple Juice

  • Provides energy from natural carbohydrates.
  • Contains water that contributes to hydration.
  • Provides small amounts of certain vitamins and minerals.
  • Can be enjoyed as part of a balanced diet.
Remember: Apple juice usually contains less fiber and can contain more sugar per serving than eating a whole apple.

How Is Apple Juice Made?

  1. Apples are picked from trees.
  2. The apples are washed and inspected.
  3. They are crushed or pressed to extract the juice.
  4. The juice may be filtered and pasteurized.
  5. It is packaged and prepared for drinking.

Fun Facts About Apples

  • There are thousands of apple varieties worldwide.
  • Apples are members of the rose family.
  • Apple juice can be made from different apple varieties.
  • Apple juice is used in smoothies, sauces, and recipes.
