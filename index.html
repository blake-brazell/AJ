01 — The Basics

What Makes Apple Juice So Special?

Apple juice is made by extracting liquid from fresh apples. Its sweet flavor, golden color, and refreshing taste make it a popular drink around the world.

Depending on how it's processed, apple juice can be clear and filtered or cloudy with more of the fruit's natural components remaining.

From breakfast tables to smoothies and recipes, apple juice is a versatile drink that fits many occasions.

Why People Love Apple Juice

Fruit Nutrients

Versatile Flavor

Enjoy it chilled, blend it into smoothies, or use it in recipes.

💡 Good to know: Whole apples generally provide more fiber than apple juice. Juice can also contain substantial amounts of sugar, even when no sugar is added. Enjoy it in sensible portions as part of a balanced diet.
